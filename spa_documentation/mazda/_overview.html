What Mazda is

Mazda is the live Letta document-verification agent for ROL Finances. Scanned receipts, invoices, statements, and other finance documents are dispatched to her to classify, check for repeats, categorize, store, verify, and report.

The live agent is agent-6b536cf4-ec88-4290-b595-fed21d14bd8e. Her durable operating knowledge is authored in memfs system/*.md files and projected into read-only Letta memory blocks. The attached knowledge currently covers intake procedures, database structure, report contracts, scanner recovery, environment details, and statement-specific rules.

Document-intake contract

The current happy path is: load the active wrapper revision; classify unknown images; check vendor identity and repeats; categorize; store; record structured intake evidence; judge the trace; propose an improvement after failure; and always send the dashboard callback, including when the document was already stored.
