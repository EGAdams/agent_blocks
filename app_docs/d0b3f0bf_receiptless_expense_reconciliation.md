# Expense reconciler: no receipt expected

`lancedb_memory/finance_team/roles.py` now directs `ROLES['expense_reconciler']` to classify receiptless expenses **before** looking for missing receipts. This keeps transfers, loan/commercial-line payments, numbered checks, interest/finance charges, and listed recurring-subscription vendors out of the UNACCOUNTED bucket, so that bucket focuses on expenses that could plausibly have a receipt. Matching is case-insensitive and tolerates minor punctuation/whitespace differences. The subscription-token list is explicit and extendable; additions discovered during a scan must be explained in the report.

Only non-excluded rows undergo the existing receipt check: matching date, amount within $0.01, and an overlapping vendor/description token. The single report remains at `{REPO_ROOT}/reports/unaccounted_expenses_report.md`. Its Summary now includes excluded counts and categories plus the unaccounted share of **remaining** rows by method; Unaccounted Expenses contains only eligible unmatched rows; the new No Receipt Expected table lists excluded rows and their matched category for audit. Caveats now include coincidental pattern matches. The role's final reply includes scanned, excluded, and unaccounted counts. The read-only/single-report restriction remains, with its step reference updated to step 6.
